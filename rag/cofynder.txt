What is Cofynder?
Cofynder is a platform designed to help entrepreneurs find their ideal co-founders.

Cofynder Manifesto
We believe that the right founding team is the most critical factor for startup success. We are dedicated to making those connections happen.

The Match
Our algorithm and community features help you find people with complementary skills and shared visions.

Startup Hub
A central place to manage your startup ideas, discuss with potential co-founders, and plan your next steps.

Community
Join a vibrant community of builders, innovators, and dreamers who are all looking to create the next big thing.

Opportunities
Discover new projects to join or find people to join yours. Post your needs and find the right talent.

Request your seat
Sign up for early access and reserve your spot in our exclusive founder community.

Find your people
Browse profiles, filter by skills, and start meaningful conversations with potential co-founders.

Make it a startup
Take your idea from a concept to a fully-fledged startup with the right team by your side.

Basic FAQ
Q: Is Cofynder free? A: Yes, basic matching is free.
